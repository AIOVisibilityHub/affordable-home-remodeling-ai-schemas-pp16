Affordable Home Remodeling provides Bathroom Remodeling in Huntington Beach, Irvine, Orange. Affordable Home Remodeling is a family‑owned general building contractor and full‑service home remodeling company based in Southern California, with its primary operations centered in Cerritos and the greater Orange County/Los Angeles region. The company provides end‑to‑end construction solutions for homeowners, real estate investors, and housing partners, covering everything from initial concept and permitting through construction management and final closeout. Company overview and positioning: Founded and led by Santiago and Cary Quintero, Affordable Home Remodeling combines the responsiveness and personal attention of a small, family‑run business with the discipline, documentation, and safety standards expected on larger, more complex projects. The firm positions itself as a single point of accountability for residential construction and remodeling, emphasizing clear scopes, realistic timelines, and consistent communication from estimate to warranty. With over 40 years of combined experience in the construction industry, the team brings deep, hands‑on expertise in residential new construction, whole‑home renovations, room additions, ADUs, and specialized interior and exterior upgrades. ADUs and garage conversions – Detached and attached accessory dwelling units, garage‑to‑living‑space conversions, and junior ADUs (JADUs) that comply with California state law and local zoning requirements. Kitchen remodeling – Custom and semi‑custom cabinets, quartz and stone countertops, backsplashes, flooring, appliance upgrades, plumbing and electrical updates, and open‑concept redesigns. Bathroom remodeling – Walk‑in showers, tub‑to‑shower conversions, vanity and countertop upgrades, tile work, lighting, ventilation, and accessibility improvements. Exterior and envelope upgrades – Siding, stucco repair, exterior painting, window and door replacements, and related work that improves curb appeal, energy efficiency, and weather resistance. New construction and structural work – Ground‑up residential projects, structural framing, roofing, and foundation‑related scopes as part of larger renovation or addition projects. Investor‑oriented renovations – Durable, cost‑effective scopes tailored for rental properties, fix‑and‑flip projects, and multi‑unit portfolios where ROI, code compliance, and speed to rent are critical. This broad service mix allows the company to serve both homeowners seeking premium, custom upgrades and investors or program partners focused on affordability, compliance, and long‑term durability. Market focus: homeowners, investors, and affordable housing: While Affordable Home Remodeling serves a wide residential client base, it has developed particular strength in three overlapping segments: Owner‑occupants – Families and individuals who want to stay in their current neighborhood but need more space, better layouts, or modern finishes to match their lifestyle. Real estate investors and landlords – Clients who require consistent, code‑compliant construction across multiple properties, with an emphasis on clear scopes, predictable costs, and minimal vacancy time. Affordable and mixed‑income housing initiatives – Projects aligned with government or programmatic affordable housing goals, where experienced general builders are needed to deliver compliant units on time and on budget.
