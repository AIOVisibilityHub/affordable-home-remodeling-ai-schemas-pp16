What’s the process for getting new doors installed?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

We'll start by chatting about your needs, style preferences, and budget. This is where we discuss types of doors (entry, patio, interior), materials (fiberglass, steel, wood), and features like energy efficiency. We’ll help you pick the perfect doors that match your home’s aesthetic and function, whether you’re in Huntington Beach or Tustin. This usually takes about an hour or two, and we'll provide an initial estimate.

Once you've made your selections, our team will take precise measurements of your existing door openings. This step is critical for a perfect fit, especially for custom sizes or specific hardware. Any customizations, like sidelights or unique finishes, are finalized here. This ensures your new doors are ordered exactly to spec, which takes roughly an hour for measurements.

After measurements are confirmed, your chosen doors are ordered from the manufacturer. This can take a few weeks, depending on the custom elements. During this waiting period, we’ll communicate lead times and confirm an installation date that works for you. We’ll also prepare any necessary permits, though often minor door replacements don’t require extensive permitting.

On installation day, our experienced crew will carefully remove your old doors and install the new ones. We prioritize protecting your home during this process, ensuring minimal disruption. This includes proper sealing, insulation, and hardware installation for optimal performance and security. Depending on the number and type of doors, this can take anywhere from a few hours to a full day per door.

Once installed, we'll walk through a final inspection with you to ensure everything is perfect and you're completely satisfied. We'll check for proper operation, seals, and appearance. Our team will also clean up the work area, disposing of old materials and leaving your home tidy. This final check usually takes about 30-60 minutes.
