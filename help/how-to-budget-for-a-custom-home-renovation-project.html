How to budget for a custom home renovation project?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Define Your Vision. Start by clearly outlining what you want to achieve with your renovation. The more specific you are about layouts, finishes, and features, the better. This usually takes a few weeks of brainstorming and research.

Step 2: Get Professional Estimates. Contact experienced contractors, like us at Affordable Home Remodeling, to get detailed quotes. We'll help you understand the costs associated with your specific custom design, covering everything from materials to labor. This step is crucial and can take 2-4 weeks.

Step 3: Account for Soft Costs. Remember to budget for things like architectural drawings, permits, and engineering fees. These aren't construction costs but are essential for a compliant project. Typically, these are a percentage of your overall project and can take several weeks to finalize.

Step 4: Create a Contingency Fund. Always set aside 10-20% of your total budget for unexpected issues. Things can pop up once you start opening up walls, and having this buffer prevents stress and delays. This fund is managed throughout the project duration.

Step 5: Prioritize and Be Flexible. If your initial estimates exceed your budget, be prepared to make some compromises. Decide what’s most important to you and what you can adjust without sacrificing your vision. This is an ongoing conversation with your contractor.
