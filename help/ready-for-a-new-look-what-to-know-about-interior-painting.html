Ready for a New Look? What to Know About Interior Painting

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Giving your home a fresh coat of interior paint is one of the quickest ways to dramatically change its feel and boost its appeal. But before you dive in, there are a few things that are really good to know. First, choosing the right paint type and finish is more important than you might think.

Different rooms benefit from different paints – an eggshell finish might be great for living areas, but you might want a satin or semi-gloss in a bathroom or kitchen for better durability and moisture resistance. Second, don't skimp on surface preparation. A professional painter will tell you that the secret to a flawless finish is in the prep work: cleaning, sanding, patching, and priming are essential.

Skipping these steps means your new paint won't look as good or last as long. Third, think about color flow throughout your home. While each room can have its own personality, having a cohesive color palette can make your entire home feel more harmonious and spacious.

Consider how colors transition from one space to another. Finally, while it might seem like a simple DIY project, professional painters bring a level of speed, precision, and clean-up that's hard to match. They also have the right tools for the job, ensuring a streak-free, even coat.
