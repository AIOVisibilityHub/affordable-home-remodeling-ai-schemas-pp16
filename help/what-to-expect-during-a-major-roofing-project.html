What to Expect During a Major Roofing Project

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Initial Consultation and Inspection. This is where we start. Our team will come out to your property in areas like Huntington Beach, Irvine, or anywhere in our service area, to conduct a thorough assessment of your existing roof. We’ll discuss your needs, concerns, and preferences, providing a transparent estimate and outlining the scope of work. This stage is crucial for setting clear expectations and answering all your questions.

Step 2: Material Selection and Permitting. With our guidance, you'll select the appropriate roofing materials that suit your home's style and your budget. We handle all the necessary permits and ensure compliance with California building codes and local requirements. This ensures your project moves forward without legal hiccups and meets all safety standards. Our local knowledge of building departments is a big plus here.

Step 3: Preparation and Site Protection. Before any work begins, our crews will take steps to protect your property. This includes covering landscaping, driveways, and any other vulnerable areas to shield them from debris. We emphasize job-site safety protocols to protect clients, workers, and properties throughout the process.

Step 4: Old Roof Removal. This is typically the loudest and messiest part. The existing roof layers are stripped down to the decking. Our team ensures proper disposal of all debris, keeping the work area as clean as possible. This stage moves quickly, and you might notice a lot of activity on your roof.

Step 5: Decking Inspection and Repairs. Once the old roof is off, we inspect the roof deck for any damage, such as rot or structural weaknesses. Any necessary repairs are made at this point to ensure a solid foundation for your new roof. This is a critical step for the longevity and integrity of the new installation.

Step 6: Underlayment and Flashing Installation. A protective underlayment is installed over the decking, acting as a secondary barrier against moisture. Flashing around chimneys, vents, and other penetrations is also installed or replaced to prevent leaks in vulnerable areas. These components are vital for your roof's waterproofing.

Step 7: New Roofing Material Installation. This is where your new roof starts to take shape! Whether it's shingles, tiles, or another material, our skilled team precisely installs the chosen roofing, following manufacturer specifications and industry best practices. Our combined 40 years of experience means a high-quality, durable installation.

Step 8: Final Cleanup and Inspection. Once the installation is complete, a thorough cleanup of the entire property is performed, including magnetic sweeps for stray nails. We then conduct a final inspection to ensure every detail meets our high standards and your satisfaction. We'll walk you through the finished project, explain any warranty details, and address any remaining questions.
