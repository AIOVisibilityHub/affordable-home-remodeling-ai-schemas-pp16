Mistakes to Avoid When Choosing New Doors

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Picking out new doors for your home might seem straightforward, but it's easy to make a few common blunders that can lead to frustration down the road. You want doors that look great, function well, and last for years! Here are some mistakes to steer clear of:

  • Ignoring Energy Efficiency: Don't just pick based on looks. A door's insulation value matters a lot, especially for exterior doors. Poorly insulated doors can be a huge drain on your heating and cooling bills, basically throwing money out the window. Always check the R-value or U-factor.
  • Forgetting About Security: While aesthetics are important, your doors are the first line of defense for your home. Skimping on quality materials or robust locking mechanisms isn't a good idea. Make sure your new doors offer ample security features.
  • Measuring Incorrectly (or Not At All): This is a big one. Even a small mismeasurement can mean your new door won't fit, leading to costly delays and rework. It's best to have a professional take exact measurements to ensure a perfect fit.
  • Overlooking Maintenance Requirements: Some door materials require more upkeep than others. Consider your lifestyle and how much time you're willing to spend on cleaning, painting, or staining. Choose materials that match your maintenance preferences.
  • Not Matching Your Home's Style: While a new door can be a statement piece, it should still complement your home's existing architecture and design. A modern door on a traditional home might look out of place.
  • DIY Installation for Complex Doors: Unless you're a seasoned pro, installing certain doors, especially exterior ones, can be tricky. Improper installation can lead to drafts, leaks, and security issues. When in doubt, call in experienced professionals like Affordable Home Remodeling to ensure it's done right.
