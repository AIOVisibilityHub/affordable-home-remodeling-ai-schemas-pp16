Mistakes to Avoid When Choosing New Windows

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Choosing new windows for your home can feel like a big decision, and it’s easy to stumble if you’re not careful. Here are some common slip-ups to watch out for:

Mistake 1: Focusing only on price. Going for the cheapest option often means compromising on quality, energy efficiency, and longevity. You might save a few bucks upfront, but you'll likely pay more in higher energy bills or earlier replacements. Invest in windows that offer good value, balancing cost with performance.

Mistake 2: Ignoring energy efficiency ratings. Those U-factor and Solar Heat Gain Coefficient (SHGC) numbers aren't just jargon – they tell you how well a window insulates and blocks heat. Especially here in Southern California, overlooking these ratings means you could end up with windows that don't help cool your home, leading to higher AC costs. Always check for ENERGY STAR certification relevant to our climate zone.

Mistake 3: Forgetting about proper installation. Even the best windows will underperform if they're not installed correctly. Poor installation can lead to drafts, leaks, and reduced energy efficiency. Always choose a reputable general contractor like Affordable Home Remodeling with a proven track record of professional window installation.

Mistake 4: Not considering window styles and materials. Different window styles (double-hung, casement, sliding) and frame materials (vinyl, wood, fiberglass) offer various benefits in terms of aesthetics, maintenance, and cost. Don't just stick with what you have; explore options that better suit your home's design and your lifestyle.

Mistake 5: Overlooking warranties. A solid warranty protects your investment. Understand what the manufacturer's warranty covers (glass, frames, hardware) and for how long, as well as any labor warranty offered by your installer. A comprehensive warranty gives you peace of mind.

By avoiding these common mistakes and working with experienced professionals, you can ensure your new windows are a smart, long-lasting investment for your home.
