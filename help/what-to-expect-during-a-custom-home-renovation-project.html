What to Expect During a Custom Home Renovation Project

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Design. This is where we really get to know your ideas, needs, and budget. We'll discuss your vision, explore design possibilities, and provide a preliminary scope. This phase focuses on understanding your goals for the custom transformation.

Step 2: Detailed Planning & Permitting. Once we have a clear concept, we move into detailed design, material selections, and architectural plans. This is also when we handle all the necessary permits with local authorities, ensuring everything is compliant with building codes. This can take some time, but it's crucial for a smooth project.

Step 3: Demolition (if applicable). Depending on the scope, the project might start with carefully controlled demolition. This prepares the space for its new layout and features, making way for the custom elements to come.

Step 4: Framing & Rough-Ins. This phase involves structural work like new framing, followed by the installation of new electrical wiring, plumbing lines, and HVAC ductwork behind the walls. All of this foundational work is inspected to meet safety and code standards.

Step 5: Finishes & Features. This is often the most exciting part, as your vision starts to materialize! We install custom cabinetry, countertops, flooring, paint, light fixtures, and all the unique touches that make your renovation truly custom. This includes all the details you’ve carefully selected.

Step 6: Final Walkthrough & Punch List. Before wrapping up, we'll do a thorough walkthrough with you. We'll identify any last-minute adjustments or touch-ups needed, creating a 'punch list' to ensure every detail meets your satisfaction before final payment. Our goal is your complete happiness with your newly renovated space. We stand behind our work with labor warranties.
