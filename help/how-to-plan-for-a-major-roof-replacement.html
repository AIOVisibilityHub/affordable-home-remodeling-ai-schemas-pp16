How to Plan for a Major Roof Replacement

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Before anything else, reach out to a few reputable contractors like Affordable Home Remodeling. Don't just look at the bottom line; make sure their proposals clearly outline the scope of work, materials used, and warranty information. Discuss different roofing materials—like asphalt shingles, tiles, or metal—and their pros and cons for your specific climate and budget. This step usually takes a week or two, depending on how quickly you get appointments and quotes.

Once you've chosen a contractor, carefully review the contract. It should include payment schedules, project timelines, cleanup procedures, and what happens with unexpected issues. This is also when you'll finalize your material choices, colors, and any specific requests. This phase can take a few days to a week to ensure everything is clear before signing.

Before the crew arrives, do some prep work around your home. Move vehicles from the driveway and ensure there's clear access to the roof. Protect landscaping by covering plants or moving outdoor furniture. Remove delicate items from walls inside your home, especially in the attic, as vibrations during installation can cause them to fall. This prep work should happen a day or two before the project begins.

During the actual replacement, expect some noise and debris. Keep children and pets away from the work area for safety. Communicate regularly with your project manager about any concerns or questions. Professional teams will ensure proper cleanup at the end of each day, but it’s always good to be aware. A typical roof replacement can take anywhere from a few days to a week, depending on the roof's size and complexity, and weather conditions.

After the work is done, do a thorough walk-around with your contractor. Check for any remaining debris and ensure the new roof looks correct. Ask about maintenance tips and confirm you have all warranty documents. Address any minor touch-ups or concerns immediately. This final check usually takes an hour or two on the last day of the project, followed by any necessary follow-ups in the weeks after. By following these steps, you’ll be well on your way to a sturdy, beautiful new roof that protects your home for years to come.
