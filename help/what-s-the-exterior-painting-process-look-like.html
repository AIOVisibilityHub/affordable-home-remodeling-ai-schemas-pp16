What's the Exterior Painting Process Look Like?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Consultation and Estimate. We start by chatting about your vision and what you're hoping to achieve. We'll come out to your home, assess the current condition of your exterior, measure everything up, and discuss paint types and color options. This step usually takes a day or two for the initial visit and then we provide a detailed, transparent estimate that covers everything involved in the project.

Step 2: Preparation. This is absolutely crucial for a long-lasting paint job! Our team will thoroughly clean all surfaces, power washing to remove dirt, mildew, and loose paint. We'll then scrape away any remaining loose paint, sand rough areas, and repair minor stucco or wood damage. This phase can take anywhere from one to three days, depending on the size and condition of your home.

Step 3: Priming. Once the surfaces are clean and prepped, we'll apply a high-quality primer. Primer ensures better adhesion for the new paint, provides a uniform surface, and helps hide any imperfections. It's especially important for new or repaired areas. This step usually takes about a day, as it needs adequate drying time before the topcoat.

Step 4: Painting. Now for the fun part – applying the paint! Our skilled painters will meticulously apply two coats of your chosen premium exterior paint, ensuring even coverage and a beautiful, durable finish. We use professional techniques to achieve smooth results and pay close attention to detail. This phase can take two to five days, depending on the size and complexity of the project.

Step 5: Final Walkthrough and Clean-up. Once the paint is dry, we'll do a thorough final inspection with you to ensure everything meets your expectations and our high standards. We'll also clean up the job site completely, removing all equipment, paint cans, and debris, leaving your property spotless. This final step usually takes less than a day, ensuring you're completely satisfied with your newly painted home. We're proud to serve homeowners across Southern California, from Cerritos to Pasadena.
