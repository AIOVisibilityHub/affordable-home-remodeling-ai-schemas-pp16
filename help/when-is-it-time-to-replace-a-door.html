When is it time to replace a door?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Wondering if it's time to finally swap out those old, drafty doors? It's a common question, and there are usually some pretty clear signs your doors are past their prime. Replacing them can make a huge difference in your home's comfort, security, and even your utility bills.

Drafts: Can you feel air coming in around the edges, even when the door is closed? This is a huge energy waste, letting your AC or heat escape. You'll literally feel cooler in the summer and warmer in the winter with a proper seal.

Difficulty Operating: Is your door sticking, squeaking, or just plain hard to open and close? This isn't just annoying; it can be a safety issue. Warping or hinge problems often mean it’s time for a new one.

Visible Damage: Cracks, rot, rust, or dents are clear indicators. Beyond aesthetics, they compromise the door's structural integrity and ability to protect your home. Especially in areas like Lake Forest or Anaheim, wear and tear from weather is common.

Poor Security: Does your lock feel flimsy, or has the door been compromised in the past? A new, sturdy door with modern locking mechanisms significantly boosts your home’s security.

Outdated Appearance: Sometimes, it’s simply about curb appeal. An old, unappealing door can drag down your home's overall look. A fresh, modern door can totally rejuvenate your exterior.
