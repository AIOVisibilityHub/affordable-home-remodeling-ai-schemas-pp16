Is It Time to Replace My Exterior Doors?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Wondering if your front door or patio doors have seen better days? It's a common question, and there are usually a few clear signals it's time for an upgrade. If you feel a draft around your doorframes, even when they're closed, that's a big red flag indicating poor insulation and energy loss.

Visible damage like cracks, warping, or rotting on wooden doors, or dents and rust on metal ones, also scream 'replace me!' Beyond aesthetics, if your door is hard to open or close, squeaks excessively, or its lock mechanism feels flimsy, it’s not only a nuisance but also a security risk. Plus, an old, tired door can really drag down your home's curb appeal.
