Why isn't my door closing right?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super frustrating when a door doesn't close properly, isn't it? Often, this can point to a few different issues. Maybe the door frame has shifted slightly, especially in older homes, or humidity is causing the wood to swell.

Sometimes, the hinges might be loose, or the door itself could be warped. Over time, doors can just get out of alignment from daily use. If you're noticing drafts, difficulty locking, or a door that scrapes against the frame, it's definitely time to take a closer look.

Trying to force it or doing a quick fix might not solve the underlying problem. It's often best to get a professional eye on it, especially if it's an exterior door affecting your home's security or insulation.
