This is where you meet with a general contractor, like us at Affordable Home Remodeling, to discuss your vision, budget, and local ADU regulations in areas like Irvine or Pasadena. We'll assess your garage's condition and determine if your project is feasible given your property's specific characteristics and zoning rules. This phase clarifies your goals and sets realistic expectations for the project ahead.

Once the concept is solid, architects and designers will create detailed plans for your ADU, including floor plans, elevations, and structural drawings. This stage also involves selecting finishes and materials. Your contractor works with the design team to ensure everything meets code requirements and your aesthetic preferences, making sure the design is practical and compliant.

With plans finalized, your contractor submits them to the local building department (e.g., in Cerritos or Lake Forest) for review and approval. This can sometimes be the longest phase, as officials check for compliance with all state and local building codes, including specific ADU regulations. Having a contractor familiar with these processes, like Affordable Home Remodeling, can really help streamline things.

This is where the magic happens! Your general contractor manages the entire construction process, coordinating tradespeople for demolition, framing, plumbing, electrical, insulation, drywall, flooring, and finishes. Regular inspections will occur throughout this phase to ensure work meets approved plans and safety standards. Communication is key here, with your contractor keeping you informed of progress.

After construction is complete, the city will conduct final inspections to ensure everything is up to code. Once approved, you'll receive your Certificate of Occupancy, officially making your garage a legal ADU. Your contractor will also do a final walk-through with you to ensure your complete satisfaction. This marks the exciting moment you can finally move in or rent out your new space!
