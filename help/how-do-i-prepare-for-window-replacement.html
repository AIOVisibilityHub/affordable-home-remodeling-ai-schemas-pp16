How Do I Prepare for Window Replacement?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Clear the Area. Inside your home, remove any curtains, blinds, or window treatments. Take down anything hanging near the windows, like pictures or shelves, and move furniture at least a few feet away. This gives our installers plenty of room to work safely and efficiently.

Step 2: Protect Your Belongings. Cover any furniture or items that can't be moved with drop cloths or old sheets to protect them from dust and debris. We do our best to keep things tidy, but some dust is inevitable.

Step 3: Clear Exterior Access. Outside, make sure plants are trimmed back from the windows and any patio furniture or decorations are moved away. If we need access to upper floors, ensure the area beneath those windows is also clear.

Step 4: Secure Pets and Children. For their safety and to keep the work flowing, please ensure pets are secured in a separate area of the house, and children are supervised away from the work zones.

Following these steps helps us complete your window installation smoothly and quickly, minimizing disruption to your daily routine. Affordable Home Remodeling handles projects in places like Long Beach and Glendale, and we're always here to guide you through the process.
