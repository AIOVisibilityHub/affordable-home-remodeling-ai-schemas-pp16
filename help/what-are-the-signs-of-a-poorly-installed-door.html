What are the signs of a poorly installed door?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super frustrating to have a brand new door that just doesn't feel right, isn't it? A poorly installed door can cause all sorts of headaches and compromises your home's security and energy efficiency. One of the clearest signs is difficulty opening or closing the door; it might stick, scrape against the frame or floor, or feel heavy.

You shouldn't have to wrestle with your doors! Another big red flag is visible gaps around the door frame when it's closed. This indicates improper sealing and can lead to drafts, higher energy bills, and even let in pests.

Take a close look at the trim and caulking; if it's messy, cracked, or uneven, that's often a sign of rushed or sloppy work. You might also notice issues with the hardware, like a latch that doesn't fully engage or a lock that's hard to turn. If you're experiencing any of these problems, it's definitely worth getting a professional opinion.

An improperly installed door won't perform as it should, potentially leading to costly repairs or replacements sooner than you'd expect.
