My doors look worn out – what should I do?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

If your home's doors are showing their age, looking faded, peeling, or just not sealing properly anymore, you're probably wondering what the next steps are. A worn-out door doesn't just look bad; it can also affect your home's energy efficiency and security. Getting them replaced is a great way to refresh your home's appearance and functionality.

First, think about which doors are causing the most trouble. Is it the front door that's letting in drafts, or perhaps a sliding glass door that's sticky and hard to open? Prioritizing these can help you manage your project.

Consider materials that suit Southern California's climate – you want something durable that can handle the sun and occasional dampness. Many homeowners in areas like Cerritos or Irvine opt for fiberglass or steel entry doors for their longevity and energy efficiency. You also want to think about the style.

A new door can really transform your home's curb appeal, so pick something that complements your architecture. And definitely don't forget about professional installation! Even the best door won't perform well if it's not installed correctly.
