When should I consider replacing my old roof?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about a new roof can feel like a huge undertaking, but knowing when it's time to replace yours can save you from bigger headaches and costs down the road. First off, age is a big factor; most asphalt shingle roofs last around 20-25 years. If yours is getting up there, even if it looks okay, it might be time for a professional inspection.

Next, look for visible signs of wear and tear: curling, cracked, or missing shingles, especially after high winds, are clear indicators. Granules collecting in your gutters are another tell-tale sign that your shingles are deteriorating. Sagging spots on your roof could point to structural damage from water, which is definitely something to address ASAP.

Don't forget about your energy bills; an old, inefficient roof can let a lot of heat escape or enter, making your HVAC system work overtime. If you're noticing any of these issues, or if you're planning a major home remodel or addition, it's smart to have a general contractor evaluate your roof. We can give you an honest assessment and discuss your options for a durable, long-lasting solution that fits your home and budget.

It's an investment, but a solid roof protects your whole home.
