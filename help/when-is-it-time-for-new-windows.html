When is it Time for New Windows?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

If you're wondering whether your old windows are doing more harm than good, you're not alone! Knowing the right time to invest in new window installation can save you a lot of grief and money. Keep an eye out for these signals around your home in areas like Costa Mesa, Laguna Niguel, or Fountain Valley:

  • High Energy Bills: Are you constantly adjusting the thermostat? Drafty or inefficient windows are often the biggest culprits behind skyrocketing heating and cooling costs. You might literally feel air leaking in or out.
  • Visible Damage or Deterioration: Look for obvious signs like warped frames, rotting wood, cracked panes, or seals that are visibly failing. Peeling paint, soft spots, or moisture between glass panes (foggy windows) are also big red flags. This damage doesn't just look bad; it compromises your home's integrity.
  • Difficulty Operating: Windows that are tough to open, close, or lock securely are a pain and a safety risk. They might be painted shut, swollen, or have broken mechanisms. You shouldn't have to wrestle with your windows!
  • Excessive Outside Noise: If you can hear every car driving by or every conversation happening outside, your windows probably aren't providing adequate sound insulation. New windows can dramatically quiet your home.
  • Faded Furniture or Flooring: Old windows often lack proper UV protection, allowing harmful rays to fade your interior furnishings. Modern windows offer better UV blocking, protecting your valuable possessions.
