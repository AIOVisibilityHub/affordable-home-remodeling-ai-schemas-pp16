What Happens During a Window Replacement Project?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Initial Consultation and Selection. This is where we kick things off! We'll sit down with you to discuss your needs, preferences, and budget.

We'll explore different window styles (like single-hung, double-hung, casement, or picture windows), materials (vinyl, wood, fiberglass), and energy-efficiency features. We'll also take precise measurements of your existing window openings. This step is all about making sure you get the perfect windows that match your home's aesthetics and your personal requirements.

This can take a few hours for the initial meeting, followed by a few days for our team to prepare a detailed estimate based on your choices.

Step 2: Order and Preparation. Once you've made your selections and approved the estimate, we place the order for your custom windows. During this time, which can range from a few weeks to over a month depending on the manufacturer and customization, we'll coordinate all logistics. We'll also help you prepare your home for installation, discussing things like moving furniture away from windows and protecting valuables. This ensures everything is ready for a smooth and efficient installation day. Our project manager will keep you updated on delivery timelines.

Step 3: Removal of Old Windows. On installation day, our experienced crew will carefully remove your old windows. This involves detaching the frames, sashes, and any surrounding trim that needs to be replaced. We always take great care to minimize disruption to your home and protect the surrounding areas from dust and debris. We’re quick and efficient, ensuring the opening is ready for your new windows without unnecessary exposure to the elements. This usually takes less than an hour per window, depending on its size and type.

Step 4: Installation of New Windows. With the old windows out, our team installs the new ones, ensuring they are perfectly level, plumb, and square. We use high-quality sealants and insulation to prevent drafts and maximize energy efficiency. Proper installation is key to performance, so we take our time to do it right. We'll also install any new interior and exterior trim as needed, making sure everything looks clean and finished. This is the most labor-intensive part of the process, often taking a full day or more for multiple windows.

Step 5: Finishing Touches and Cleanup. After the new windows are securely in place and sealed, we move on to the finishing touches. This includes caulking, weatherstripping, and thorough testing of each window to ensure smooth operation.

We then conduct a complete cleanup of the work area, removing all debris and old window materials. We want to leave your home cleaner than we found it! Finally, we'll walk you through the new windows, explain their features, and answer any questions you might have about care and maintenance.

The entire installation and cleanup can be completed in just a day or two for most projects, leaving you with beautiful, efficient new windows.
