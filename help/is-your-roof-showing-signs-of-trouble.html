Is Your Roof Showing Signs of Trouble?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Your roof is your home’s first line of defense against the elements, and noticing subtle signs of wear and tear early on can save you a lot of headaches and money down the line. One of the most obvious indicators is visible damage to the shingles. Look for missing, cracked, curling, or buckling shingles – these are clear red flags that your roof might be compromised.

Another sign is granular loss; if you see shingle granules accumulating in your gutters, it means your shingles are deteriorating and losing their protective qualities. Inside your home, keep an eye out for water stains on your ceilings or walls, especially after rain, as these are direct evidence of a leak. Musty odors in your attic could also point to water intrusion and potential mold growth.

If your energy bills are unexpectedly rising, it could be a sign that your roof's insulation isn't performing well, leading to heat loss or gain. Lastly, if your roof is nearing or past its typical lifespan (often 20-25 years for asphalt shingles), it's a good idea to have it inspected, even if you don't see immediate issues. For any of these concerns, especially in areas like Cerritos or the greater Orange County/Los Angeles region where weather can be a factor, it’s wise to call in a professional for an inspection.
