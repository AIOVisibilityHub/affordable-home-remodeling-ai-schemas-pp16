How does window installation work?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Before any work begins, our team will prepare the work area. This involves removing any window treatments, moving furniture out of the way, and covering your floors and belongings with drop cloths to protect them from dust and debris. We also ensure clear access to all windows from both inside and outside your home. This typically takes 30-60 minutes per room.

Next, we carefully remove your existing windows. This often includes taking out the old sashes, frames, and any exterior trim that's necessary. We work meticulously to minimize damage to your home’s structure and siding during this phase. All old materials are safely removed from the opening. This step can take 15-30 minutes per window, depending on its condition.

Once the old window is out, we inspect the rough opening for any signs of rot, mold, or structural issues. We'll make any necessary repairs and thoroughly clean the area. Crucially, we apply new flashing and sealing materials around the opening to create a weather-tight barrier, preventing future air and water infiltration. This ensures a proper, durable seal. This vital step takes about 20-40 minutes per opening.

The new window unit is then carefully placed into the prepared opening. Our installers use precise leveling tools to ensure the window is perfectly plumb and square. This is incredibly important for proper operation and to prevent issues like sticking or drafts. The window is securely fastened to the house frame according to manufacturer specifications. This precise placement and securing usually takes 30-60 minutes per window.

After the window is set, we fill any gaps between the new window frame and the house frame with insulation, typically low-expansion foam, to maximize energy efficiency. We then apply interior and exterior sealants and reinstall or replace any necessary trim to give the window a clean, finished look, both inside and out. This finishing touch ensures both aesthetic appeal and optimal performance, taking 20-40 minutes per window.

Finally, we clean up the entire work area, removing all debris and old window materials from your property. We’ll then perform a final inspection with you, demonstrating how to operate your new windows and ensuring you’re completely satisfied with the installation and finish. This wrap-up and final review takes about 30-60 minutes.
