Your Bathroom Remodel: What to Expect from Start to Finish

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Initial Consultation and Design. This is where we chat about your vision, needs, and budget. We'll explore ideas, materials, and layout options to create a design that truly fits your style and home. This phase usually takes a few weeks, depending on design complexity and material selection, and it's all about making sure we're on the same page.

Step 2: Planning and Permitting. Once the design is approved, we handle the technical stuff like detailed plans and securing any necessary permits from local authorities. This ensures everything is up to California building codes and regulations. This part can vary, but we aim to make it as smooth as possible for you.

Step 3: Demolition and Rough-In. This is where the old bathroom comes out! We'll carefully remove existing fixtures, tiles, and anything else being replaced. Then, the rough-ins for plumbing and electrical systems are installed according to the new design. This is usually a quicker phase, maybe a few days to a week.

Step 4: Installation of New Features. Now for the exciting part! New tubs, showers, vanities, and cabinets go in. We also lay the new flooring and start on the tile work. This is where your bathroom really begins to take shape, typically spanning a couple of weeks depending on material availability and complexity.

Step 5: Finishing Touches and Final Walkthrough. We install light fixtures, faucets, mirrors, paint, and all the small details that make your bathroom perfect. Finally, we do a thorough walkthrough with you to ensure every detail meets your expectations. This last stage can take a few days. With Affordable Home Remodeling, serving areas like Irvine and Orange, we manage each step meticulously for a high-quality finish.
