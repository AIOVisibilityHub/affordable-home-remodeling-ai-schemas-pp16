How to Plan Your Home Addition Project

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Start by figuring out exactly why you need more space. Do you want an extra bedroom, a larger kitchen, or a new family room? Consider your lifestyle, future plans, and what functions the new space will serve. This initial brainstorming helps shape the entire project.

Before you get too deep into design, understand what’s permissible in your area. Regulations vary significantly, even within Southern California cities like Orange or Lake Forest. Check local building codes and zoning laws regarding setbacks, height limits, and lot coverage. This step is crucial and can save you from costly redesigns later.

Be honest about what you can afford. Get estimates for construction, materials, and don't forget 'soft costs' like architectural drawings, engineering, and permits. Always add a contingency fund (10-20%) for unexpected issues. Affordable Home Remodeling works to provide clear, transparent pricing.

Look for a general contractor with experience in home additions, especially one who understands local regulations. They can help with both design and construction, ensuring a cohesive process. Interview a few, check references, and review their portfolios. A good contractor will be licensed and insured, just like Affordable Home Remodeling.

Once you've chosen your team, finalize the architectural plans and submit them for approval to your local building department. This can take time, so patience is key. Your contractor should manage this complex process, ensuring all documentation is correct.

With permits in hand, construction can start. Your contractor will manage the timeline, materials, and subcontractors. Expect some disruption, but good communication with your team will keep you informed every step of the way, minimizing stress. We strive for consistent communication from estimate to warranty.
