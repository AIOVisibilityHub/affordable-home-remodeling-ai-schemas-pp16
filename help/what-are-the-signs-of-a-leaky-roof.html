What are the signs of a leaky roof?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Discovering a roof leak is never fun, but catching it early can save you a lot of hassle and expense. There are some pretty common signs that your roof might be letting water in, even if you don't see drips immediately. First, look for water stains on your ceilings or walls.

These can be faint at first, appearing as discolored patches that spread over time. Another big giveaway is peeling paint or bubbling plaster, especially on exterior walls or near the ceiling line – water might be seeping behind the finish. Musty odors in your attic or upper floors are also a red flag; they often signal hidden moisture and potential mold growth.

Outside, check for missing, cracked, or curled shingles, particularly after a storm. Granules from asphalt shingles accumulating in your gutters is another sign your roof is aging. If you spot any of these, it’s not something to put off.
