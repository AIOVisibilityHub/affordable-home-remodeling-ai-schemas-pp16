Door Replacement | Affordable Home Remodeling

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-05

Upgrade your home's security and style with professional door replacement services from our family-owned team. Affordable Home Remodeling provides professional door replacement to improve your home’s security, energy efficiency, and curb appeal. Faded or worn entry doors can drag down the appearance of your property, even when the structure is sound. Our team helps you select and install high-quality replacements that withstand the Southern California sun and wind.

Led by Santiago and Cary Quintero, we bring over 40 years of combined experience to every installation. We handle the entire process from measuring to final trim, ensuring a weather-tight fit that prevents energy loss. Whether you are updating a single front door for a fresh look or replacing all exterior doors for a rental property, we provide clear scopes and dependable service. Common situations: Replacing a weathered or drafty front door to improve energy efficiency.; Updating old exterior doors to enhance curb appeal before a home sale.; Upgrading entry points on an investment property to meet modern security standards.. Related services: Door Installation, Window Replacement, Exterior Painting Services, Stucco Repair, Home Renovation, Energy-Efficient Windows. Locations served: Cerritos, Huntington Beach, Irvine, Anaheim, Pasadena, Long Beach, Torrance, Newport Beach.
