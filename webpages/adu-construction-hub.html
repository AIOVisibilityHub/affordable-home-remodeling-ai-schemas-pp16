Maximize your property's potential with professional ADU construction services from Affordable Home Remodeling, Southern California's trusted general building contractor. Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs), often called granny flats or backyard cottages, are an excellent way to enhance the value and utility of your Southern California property. Affordable Home Remodeling provides end-to-end ADU design and build services, helping homeowners navigate California’s state laws and local zoning requirements. Whether you are looking for a detached unit for rental income or an attached space for family members, our team manages everything from permitting through construction management.

Our family-owned company specializes in creating functional, independent living spaces that include kitchens, bathrooms, and private entrances. With a deep understanding of local building departments in Orange County and Los Angeles, Santiago and Cary Quintero ensure that your ADU is built safely, on time, and within budget. We focus on durable, code-compliant construction that meets both homeowner lifestyle needs and investor ROI goals. Common situations: Homeowners wanting to generate monthly rental income by building a detached unit in their backyard.; Families needing a private, independent living space for aging parents or adult children.; Investors looking to add a second dwelling to a property to increase its overall market value and yield.. Related services: Detached ADU, Attached ADU, Junior ADU, Garage Conversions to ADU, ADU Design and Build, Investment Property Renovation. Locations served: Cerritos, Huntington Beach, Irvine, Orange, Yorba Linda, Mission Viejo, Newport Beach, Lake Forest, Costa Mesa, Laguna Niguel, Fountain Valley, Anaheim, Tustin, Buena Park, Los Alamitos, Pasadena, Torrance, Glendale, Burbank, Rancho Palos Verdes, South Pasadena, Palos Verdes Estates, San Marino, El Segundo, Diamond Bar, Long Beach, Downey, Whittier, Cypress.
