Ensure your home is secure and energy-efficient with professional door installation from licensed contractors. Proper door installation is essential for both the functionality and safety of your home. Affordable Home Remodeling offers expert installation for front entries, patio doors, and interior doors. Our family-owned team ensures that each door is perfectly leveled and sealed to prevent drafts, moisture intrusion, and security vulnerabilities.

We serve homeowners and real estate investors across Orange County and the Greater Los Angeles area, providing a single point of accountability for all residential construction needs. With decades of field experience, Santiago and Cary Quintero lead a team that prioritizes punctuality and clean worksites. From high-end custom entries to durable options for rental properties, we deliver professional results backed by our commitment to integrity and quality. Common situations: Installing new entry doors as part of a major home addition or renovation.; Replacing faulty interior doors to improve privacy and sound dampening.; Upgrading to a new front door to modernize a home's entryway.. Related services: Door Replacement, Home Additions, New Construction, Whole House Remodel, Window Installation, Exterior Renovation. Locations served: Cerritos, Fullerton, Lakewood, Huntington Beach, Irvine, Whittier, Downey, Mission Viejo.
