Garage Conversions to ADU | Affordable Home Remodeling

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-05

Type: CollectionPage

Turn your underutilized garage into a modern, code-compliant living space with professional garage conversion services from Affordable Home Remodeling. Converting an existing garage into an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) is one of the most cost-effective ways to add living space to your property. Affordable Home Remodeling specializes in transforming underutilized garages into fully functional studios or apartments. Because the primary structure is already in place, these conversions often require a smaller investment than ground-up detached ADUs while still providing a significant increase in property value and utility.

Our team handles the complex structural, plumbing, and electrical updates required to turn a garage into a legal living area. We ensure every conversion complies with California building codes and local municipal requirements. From Junior ADUs (JADUs) to full garage-to-living-space transformations, Santiago and Cary Quintero provide the professional oversight needed to ensure the project is safe, permitted, and finished with the high-quality craftsmanship our Southern California clients expect. Common situations: Converting an attached garage into a Junior ADU (JADU) to house a family member.; Transforming a detached two-car garage into a rental studio to offset mortgage costs.; Homeowners looking for a more affordable way to add square footage without losing backyard space.. Related services: Junior ADU, ADU Design and Build, Whole-Home Remodeling, Flooring Installation, Kitchen Remodeling, Bathroom Remodeling. Locations served: Cerritos, Huntington Beach, Irvine, Orange, Yorba Linda, Mission Viejo, Newport Beach, Lake Forest, Costa Mesa, Laguna Niguel, Fountain Valley, Anaheim, Tustin, Buena Park, Los Alamitos, Pasadena, Torrance, Glendale, Burbank, Rancho Palos Verdes, South Pasadena, Palos Verdes Estates, San Marino, El Segundo, Diamond Bar, Long Beach, Downey, Whittier, Cypress.
