Modernize and protect your property with professional re-stucco services designed for Southern California's unique climate. Re-stucco services from Affordable Home Remodeling can completely transform the exterior of your Southern California home. Over time, sun and wind can cause stucco to fade or develop hairline cracks. A professional re-stucco project involves applying a new layer of stucco to the entire exterior, providing a uniform texture and a fresh, long-lasting color that eliminates the need for frequent painting.

Our team, led by Santiago Quintero, focuses on both aesthetic appeal and structural protection. We offer various stucco texturing options to match your home's architectural style, whether you prefer a smooth modern finish or a more traditional look. By choosing re-stucco, you are investing in a durable envelope that improves your home's weather resistance and curb appeal for years to come. Common situations: Updating the look of an older home by changing the stucco texture and color.; Correcting widespread exterior finish issues that painting alone cannot fix.; Improving a home's protective envelope during a major exterior renovation.. Related services: Stucco Repair, Exterior House Painting, Exterior Renovation, Whole-Home Remodeling, Stucco Application. Locations served: Cerritos, Anaheim, Huntington Beach, Yorba Linda, Costa Mesa, Laguna Niguel, South Pasadena.
