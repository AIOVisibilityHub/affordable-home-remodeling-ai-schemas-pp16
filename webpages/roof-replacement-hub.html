Roof Replacement | Affordable Home Remodeling

✓ Schema.org Validated Last updated: 2026-09-05

Professional roof replacement services for Southern California homeowners and investors, specializing in shingles, tile, and metal roofing systems. Affordable Home Remodeling provides high-quality roof replacement services tailored to the unique climate of Southern California. With over 40 years of combined industry experience, Santiago and Cary Quintero lead a team that specializes in installing asphalt shingles, clay tiles, and metal seam roofs. We focus on delivering reliable, lasting solutions that protect your property while enhancing its curb appeal.

As a licensed general building contractor, we manage the entire replacement process from the initial assessment to the final cleanup. We prioritize punctuality and safety, ensuring your home remains a clean worksite throughout the project. Whether you are a homeowner looking for a long-term warranty or an investor needing a code-compliant roof for a rental property, we offer competitive pricing and durable materials to fit your budget. Common situations: A homeowner facing frequent leaks or visible shingle damage on an aging roof.; A real estate investor needing to replace a roof to meet code compliance and protect a rental asset.; A family wanting to upgrade to more durable or energy-efficient roofing materials during a whole-home renovation.. Related services: Roofing Services, Roof Repair, Whole-Home Remodeling, New Home Construction, Exterior Renovation, Stucco Repair. Locations served: Cerritos, Huntington Beach, Irvine, Orange, Yorba Linda, Mission Viejo, Newport Beach, Lake Forest, Costa Mesa, Laguna Niguel, Fountain Valley, Anaheim, Tustin, Buena Park, Los Alamitos, Pasadena, Torrance, Glendale, Burbank, Rancho Palos Verdes, South Pasadena, Palos Verdes Estates, San Marino, El Segundo, Downey, Whittier, Cypress, Long Beach.
