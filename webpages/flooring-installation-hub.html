Upgrade your home's interior with expert flooring installation services from the family-owned team at Affordable Home Remodeling. Affordable Home Remodeling provides professional flooring installation for residential and commercial properties throughout Southern California. Led by Santiago and Cary Quintero, our team brings decades of experience to every project, ensuring your new floors are installed with precision and care. Whether you are looking for the timeless warmth of hardwood or the resilience of modern vinyl, we help you select and install the right materials to match your lifestyle and budget.

We specialize in a wide variety of materials, including hardwood, laminate, tile, and carpet. For high-traffic areas or households with pets, we often recommend luxury vinyl planks that are waterproof, scratch-resistant, and slip-resistant. From the initial measurement to the final piece of trim, we maintain high standards of excellence to ensure a durable and beautiful result that enhances your home's value. Common situations: Choosing the right durable flooring for a high-traffic rental property.; Installing waterproof vinyl flooring in kitchens or bathrooms to prevent moisture damage.; Modernizing a home's interior by replacing old carpet with custom hardwood or laminate.. Related services: Whole-Home Remodeling, Kitchen Remodeling, Bathroom Remodeling, Hardwood Flooring Installation, Laminate Flooring Installation, Tile Flooring Installation. Locations served: Cerritos, Huntington Beach, Irvine, Pasadena, Torrance, Anaheim, Long Beach, Whittier.
