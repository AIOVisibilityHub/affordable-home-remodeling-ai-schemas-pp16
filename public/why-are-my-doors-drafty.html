Why Are My Doors Drafty?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's super frustrating when you feel a chill even with your doors closed, right? Drafty doors aren't just uncomfortable; they're also sending your energy bills soaring. Usually, it's a sign that your door's seal has seen better days or the door itself isn't fitting correctly in its frame anymore. Over time, weatherstripping can wear out, or the door might warp due to temperature changes or moisture. Sometimes, the threshold gets damaged, creating a gap at the bottom. Poor installation from years ago could also be the culprit, allowing air to leak around the frame.

When you notice drafts, it's definitely time to assess the situation. A simple fix might be new weatherstripping, but often, the problem runs deeper, suggesting the door or frame is compromised. This is especially true for older homes in places like Huntington Beach or Irvine, where homes have faced many seasons. A professional like Affordable Home Remodeling can properly diagnose why your door is drafty and suggest the best solution, whether it's a minor adjustment or a full door replacement to boost your home's energy efficiency and comfort. Don't let those drafts continue costing you money!
