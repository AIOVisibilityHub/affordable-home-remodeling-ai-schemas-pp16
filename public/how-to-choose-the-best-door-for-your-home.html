How to choose the best door for your home

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Assess Your Needs. First, think about what you need the door to do. Is it for security, insulation, aesthetic appeal, or all three? For exterior doors, security and energy efficiency are usually top priorities, especially in places like Newport Beach or Irvine where the weather can vary. For interior doors, maybe soundproofing or style is more important.

Step 2: Consider Materials. Doors come in various materials, each with pros and cons. Steel offers excellent security and durability, fiberglass is great for insulation and low maintenance, and wood provides a classic, natural look but requires more upkeep. Your choice depends on your budget, style preference, and how much wear and tear the door will see.

Step 3: Pick a Style. The door's style should complement your home's architecture. Traditional homes might suit panel doors, while modern homes might lean towards flush or glass-paneled designs. Don't forget about hardware — handles, hinges, and locks play a huge role in the door's overall look and function.

Step 4: Think About Installation. While it might seem easy to DIY, door installation can be tricky, especially for exterior doors where proper sealing and alignment are crucial for energy efficiency and security. Incorrect installation can lead to drafts, misalignment, and compromised security.
