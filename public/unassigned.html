It's super frustrating when your home's exterior paint starts looking dull or chalky way too fast, isn't it? Often, it comes down to a few key culprits that homeowners might not even realize are at play. One big one is not prepping surfaces correctly; if the old paint isn't cleaned, sanded, and primed properly, the new coat won't adhere well and will break down faster.

Also, using lower quality paint, especially in sunny Southern California, means less UV resistance, causing colors to fade quicker under intense sunlight. Another factor is the type of siding you have and if it's been painted correctly for its material. We see a lot of issues where the wrong paint type was used, leading to premature wear.

Plus, poor application, like painting in extreme temperatures or not applying enough coats, can seriously cut down on longevity. Knowing these common pitfalls can really help you understand why your paint might be failing sooner than expected. If you're scratching your head wondering why your fresh paint job isn't holding up, it's probably time to have a professional general contractor like Affordable Home Remodeling take a look.
