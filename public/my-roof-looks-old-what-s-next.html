My roof looks old, what's next?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

If your roof is looking a little worse for wear, you're probably wondering what the next steps are. An aging roof isn't just an aesthetic issue; it can be a significant vulnerability for your home. Here are some key considerations:

  • Don't wait for disaster: The biggest mistake is putting off an inspection until you have a major leak or visible damage. By then, the problem is usually much more extensive and costly to fix. Proactive checks can save you thousands.
  • Get a professional assessment: Don't try to climb up there yourself if you're not experienced. A professional roofing contractor can safely and thoroughly inspect your roof, identifying subtle signs of wear, potential weak spots, and hidden damage that might not be obvious from the ground. They can give you an honest appraisal of its remaining lifespan.
  • Understand your options: An old roof doesn't always mean a full replacement. Sometimes, repairs can extend its life significantly. Other times, a re-roofing over existing shingles is possible, or a full tear-off and replacement is necessary. Knowing the pros and cons of each, including costs and longevity, is crucial.
  • Factor in weather and efficiency: Older roofs are often less energy-efficient. A new roof can drastically improve your home's insulation, keeping it cooler in the Southern California heat and warmer during occasional cold snaps, potentially lowering your utility bills. Also, consider materials that can withstand local weather patterns.
  • Choose a reputable contractor: This is vital. You need a licensed and insured contractor who provides clear estimates, communicates well, and stands behind their work.
