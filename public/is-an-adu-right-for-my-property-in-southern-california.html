Is an ADU right for my property in Southern California?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) for your property? It's a fantastic option for many homeowners in places like Lake Forest or Mission Viejo, but it's important to understand if it’s the right fit for your specific situation. An ADU can serve multiple purposes: a rental unit for extra income, a space for aging parents or adult children, or even a dedicated home office or studio.

Your property’s size, zoning regulations, and existing structures will all play a role in determining feasibility. California state law has made ADUs much easier to build, but local requirements still vary. You'll need to consider things like access, utility connections, and privacy for both your main home and the ADU.

A well-designed ADU can significantly increase your property value and provide flexibility for your family's future needs. However, it's a major construction project, and you'll want to work with a general contractor who truly understands local ADU regulations and can manage the entire process from concept to completion.
