What is an ADU and Why Should You Consider Building One?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

An Accessory Dwelling Unit, or ADU, is essentially a secondary housing unit on a property with a single-family home. Think of it as a separate, smaller home in your backyard, or even a converted garage. These aren't just tiny homes; they can be detached structures, attached additions, or even converted existing spaces like basements or garages.

Here in Southern California, particularly in areas like Huntington Beach, Anaheim, and Pasadena, ADUs have become incredibly popular due to state laws making them easier to build. They're a fantastic solution for a variety of needs. They offer flexible living spaces, whether you need extra room for aging parents, a private space for young adult children, or a dedicated home office away from the main house.

Plus, they can be a significant source of rental income, helping homeowners with mortgage payments or providing a steady revenue stream.
