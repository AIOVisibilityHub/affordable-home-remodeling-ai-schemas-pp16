Mistakes to Avoid When Planning Your New Roof

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Planning a new roof can feel like a big project, and it's easy to overlook details that can lead to headaches down the road. But don't worry, with our decades of experience at Affordable Home Remodeling, we've helped countless homeowners in Southern California navigate this. Avoiding these common mistakes can save you time, money, and stress.

  • Ignoring the Permit Process: It's tempting to skip what seems like red tape, but proper permitting is crucial for roofing projects in California. Failing to get the right permits can lead to fines, project delays, or even requiring you to tear out newly installed work. We always handle all necessary permits to ensure compliance.
  • Choosing the Cheapest Bid: While budget is important, going with the absolute lowest bid often means compromising on quality materials, experienced labor, or proper licensing and insurance. This can result in a roof that doesn't last, causing more expensive repairs later. Look for value, not just the lowest price.
  • Not Researching Your Contractor: Your roofing contractor should be licensed, bonded, and insured, like us (License No. 736771). Always check their credentials and look for reviews. A trustworthy contractor like Affordable Home Remodeling offers written scopes, clear contracts, and stands by their work with warranties.
  • Neglecting Proper Ventilation: A new roof isn't just about shingles; good ventilation is key for its longevity and your home's energy efficiency. Poor attic ventilation can trap heat and moisture, leading to premature roof deterioration and higher utility bills. Make sure your contractor addresses this vital component.
  • Skipping the Underlayment: The underlayment is an unseen but critical layer that provides an extra barrier against moisture. Some might try to cut costs by using a subpar underlayment or skipping it entirely, but this leaves your home vulnerable to leaks. Always insist on quality underlayment.

By keeping these points in mind, you'll be much better equipped to make informed decisions for your roofing project. If you're ready to discuss your roofing needs, contact Affordable Home Remodeling today – we're here to help homeowners in Cerritos, Orange County, and Los Angeles.
