How to Replace Your Exterior Doors Like a Pro

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Selection. First, we chat about your vision! We'll visit your home in areas like Orange, Yorba Linda, or Newport Beach to measure your existing doorway and discuss door styles, materials (like wood, fiberglass, or steel), and energy efficiency ratings. We help you pick a door that matches your home’s aesthetic and your budget, ensuring it fits perfectly.

Step 2: Custom Ordering & Preparation. Once your perfect door is chosen, we place a custom order. While we wait for it to arrive, we’ll plan the installation day and make sure all necessary tools and materials are ready. This step ensures a smooth transition once the new door is on site.

Step 3: Old Door Removal. On installation day, our team carefully removes your old door, its frame, and any existing trim. We take care to protect your home’s interior and exterior during this process, minimizing disruption and mess. This often includes removing the old jamb and threshold.

Step 4: Frame Inspection & Preparation. With the old door out, we meticulously inspect the rough opening. We’ll check for any signs of rot, water damage, or structural issues and make any necessary repairs. This crucial step ensures a solid, level, and plumb foundation for your new door, preventing future problems like drafts or sticking.

Step 5: New Door Installation & Sealing. This is where the magic happens! We carefully set the new pre-hung door unit into the prepared opening, ensuring it’s perfectly plumb and level. We then fasten it securely and apply weatherstripping and sealant around the frame. This ensures maximum energy efficiency and protection from the elements, a key part of our service in places like Anaheim and Tustin.

Step 6: Finishing Touches & Cleanup. Finally, we install new trim, hardware, and any desired accessories. We adjust the door to ensure it opens and closes smoothly and latches securely. Our team then conducts a thorough cleanup, leaving your home spotless and your new door looking fantastic. We’ll also walk you through its features and care.
