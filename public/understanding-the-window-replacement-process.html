Understanding the Window Replacement Process

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Step 1: Initial Consultation and Measurement — We'll start by chatting about your needs, style preferences, and budget. Then, we take precise measurements of your existing window openings. This step is crucial for ensuring your new windows fit perfectly, preventing issues like drafts or poor insulation later on.

Step 2: Window Selection and Ordering — Based on our discussion and your home's specifics, we'll help you choose the best window types, materials, and energy-efficient features. Once you've made your selections, we place the order with our trusted suppliers, keeping you updated on estimated delivery times.

Step 3: Preparation for Installation — Before installation day, we'll confirm all the details and advise you on how to prepare your home. This usually involves clearing the area around the windows, both inside and out, and removing any blinds or curtains. We'll protect your furnishings and flooring.

Step 4: Removal of Old Windows — On installation day, our experienced team will carefully remove your old windows. We make sure to do this with minimal disruption to your home and always clean up the debris as we go, taking care of proper disposal.

Step 5: New Window Installation — The new windows are then expertly installed, leveled, and sealed to ensure maximum energy efficiency and weather protection. We pay close attention to flashing and insulation around the frames to prevent air and water leaks. This step is where our craftsmanship really shines, making sure everything is square and secure.

Step 6: Finishing Touches and Cleanup — After installation, we'll complete any necessary trim work, seal gaps, and make sure everything looks neat and tidy. We then do a thorough cleanup of the work area, leaving your home spotless. We'll also walk you through the operation of your new windows and answer any questions you have. This process is designed to be efficient and stress-free for homeowners in communities from Cerritos to Lake Forest.
