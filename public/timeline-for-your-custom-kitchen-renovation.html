Timeline for Your Custom Kitchen Renovation

This is where your vision truly starts to take shape! We’ll meet to discuss your ideas, lifestyle, budget, and desired aesthetic. We'll take detailed measurements and start sketching out design concepts. This phase involves a lot of back-and-forth to ensure the layout, materials, and overall feel align perfectly with what you envision. Our team will guide you through options for custom cabinets, countertops, flooring, and appliances, refining the design until it’s just right.

Once the conceptual design is in place, you’ll dive deeper into selecting all the finishes and fixtures. This includes finalizing specific cabinet styles, wood types, finishes, countertop slabs, backsplash tiles, plumbing fixtures, lighting, and hardware. We'll help you source and select high-quality materials that fit your design and budget. After all selections are made, the final design plans are drawn up for your approval, ready for permitting.

This phase can vary significantly depending on your specific city (like Anaheim, Newport Beach, or Tustin) and the scope of work. We handle the submission of plans to local building departments and manage the permit approval process. Simultaneously, we'll order all custom cabinetry, special-order appliances, and other long lead-time materials. It’s important to remember that custom items can take several weeks or even months to arrive, so this waiting period is crucial for a smooth construction start.

Once permits are secured and materials start arriving, the exciting part begins! We'll carefully demolish your old kitchen, removing cabinets, countertops, and flooring. Then, our team will handle the ‘rough-in’ work, which involves making any necessary structural changes, relocating or installing new plumbing lines, and updating electrical wiring to accommodate the new layout and appliances. Inspections by city officials will occur at key points during this stage.

After rough-ins pass inspection, the installation phase kicks off. Custom cabinets are installed, followed by any new drywall or wall finishes. Then, the new flooring goes in, whether it’s tile, hardwood, or another material. Each step is done with precision to ensure a flawless foundation for the rest of your kitchen.

Once cabinets are in place, templates for your custom countertops are made, and then the countertops are fabricated and installed. This is often followed by the backsplash tile installation, adding a beautiful design element. Finally, painting of walls and trim brings the whole room together with a fresh, cohesive look.

In the final stage, all your new appliances are delivered and installed, plumbing fixtures like sinks and faucets are connected, and lighting fixtures are put into place. We’ll install cabinet hardware, perform final quality checks, and do a thorough clean-up. A final walkthrough with you ensures every detail meets your high expectations. This timeline is a general guide; every custom kitchen renovation is unique. We pride ourselves on clear communication and realistic expectations, keeping you informed every step of the way to deliver the custom kitchen you've always dreamed of.
