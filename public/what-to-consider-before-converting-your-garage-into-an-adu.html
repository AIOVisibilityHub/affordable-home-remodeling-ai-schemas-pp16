What to Consider Before Converting Your Garage into an ADU

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about transforming your garage into an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) can open up a world of possibilities, whether it's for extra income, family, or just more space. But before you dive in, there are a few key things you'll want to think through. First, consider the zoning laws in your specific Southern California city; while state laws are generally favorable to ADUs, local regulations can vary.

You'll also want to assess your garage's current condition – is it structurally sound? What about electrical and plumbing needs? Understanding these upfront can save you headaches later.

Think about how the new ADU will integrate with your existing home and property layout, ensuring it feels like a natural extension. And of course, budgeting is huge; get a clear picture of costs, from design to permits to construction. It's smart to have a general contractor like Affordable Home Remodeling, who understands California's ADU rules and local permitting processes in places like Cerritos or Orange County, guide you through these initial considerations.

They can help you figure out if it's the right move for your property.
