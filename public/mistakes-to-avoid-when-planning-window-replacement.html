Mistakes to avoid when planning window replacement?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting new windows is a big decision that can significantly impact your home's comfort and value. But, like any major home improvement, there are common pitfalls you'll want to sidestep to ensure a smooth, successful project. Avoiding these mistakes can save you time, money, and a lot of headaches!

1. Going for the Cheapest Option: It’s tempting to pick the lowest bid, but bargain-basement windows often mean lower quality materials and shoddy installation. You’ll end up paying more in energy costs, repairs, or even needing to replace them sooner. Invest in quality that lasts.

2. Ignoring Energy Efficiency Ratings: Don’t just look at the price tag. Check those NFRC ratings! U-factor, Solar Heat Gain Coefficient (SHGC), and Visible Transmittance (VT) tell you how well a window insulates and manages sunlight. In sunny Southern California, a good SHGC is key for keeping your home cool.

3. Forgetting About Local Building Codes: Every city, whether it’s Long Beach or Glendale, has specific requirements for window installation. An experienced contractor knows these codes and ensures your project is compliant, preventing costly rework or fines down the line.

4. Neglecting Proper Ventilation: While new windows seal tightly, ensure you still have adequate ventilation, especially in bathrooms and kitchens. Good airflow is crucial for preventing moisture buildup and maintaining indoor air quality.

5. Skipping Professional Installation: DIY window installation is usually a bad idea unless you're a seasoned pro. Incorrect installation can lead to drafts, water leaks, operational issues, and voided warranties. Trusting a licensed general contractor like Affordable Home Remodeling ensures a perfect fit and long-term performance.

By keeping these points in mind, you’ll be much better equipped to make informed decisions and enjoy your beautiful new windows for years to come. Getting professional help from the start can prevent many of these common issues.
