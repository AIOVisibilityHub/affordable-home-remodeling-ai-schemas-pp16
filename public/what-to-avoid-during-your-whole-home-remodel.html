What to Avoid During Your Whole-Home Remodel

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Embarking on a whole-home remodel is exciting, but it's easy to make a few missteps along the way that can impact your budget and timeline. First off, don't skimp on planning. A clear vision and detailed plans upfront prevent costly changes mid-project.

Without them, you're just guessing, and that almost always leads to delays and extra expenses. Second, avoid ignoring the structural stuff. Sometimes, older homes have hidden issues with foundations or framing that need addressing before you start cosmetic changes.

Skipping these can lead to bigger, more expensive problems down the road. Third, don't try to DIY everything, especially skilled trades. Electrical, plumbing, and structural work really need licensed pros to ensure safety and code compliance.

Trying to do it all yourself often ends up costing more when a professional has to come in and fix mistakes. Fourth, resist the urge to constantly change your mind. While some flexibility is good, major design changes once construction starts can throw off the entire schedule and budget.

Try to finalize as many decisions as possible early on. Lastly, don't pick your contractor based on price alone. The cheapest bid might seem appealing, but it often comes with hidden costs or compromises on quality.
