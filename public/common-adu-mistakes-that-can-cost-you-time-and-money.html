Common ADU mistakes that can cost you time and money

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Building an ADU is an exciting investment, but it's easy to stumble into common traps if you're not careful. One big mistake is not fully understanding local zoning laws and state regulations before starting. What's allowed in one city, like Buena Park, might be different in another, like Los Alamitos, leading to costly redesigns or even project rejection.

Another pitfall is underestimating the total project cost. Many homeowners forget to factor in things like utility hookups, architectural fees, and permits, leading to budget overruns. It's crucial to get a comprehensive, detailed estimate from a reputable contractor.

Skipping proper site assessment is also a mistake; unexpected soil conditions or hidden utility lines can cause major delays. And perhaps the most common error? Not hiring an experienced, licensed, and insured contractor.

Trying to DIY or go with an unvetted builder can result in subpar work, safety issues, and no recourse if things go wrong.
