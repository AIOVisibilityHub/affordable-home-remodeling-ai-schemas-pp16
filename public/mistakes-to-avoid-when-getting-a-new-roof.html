Mistakes to Avoid When Getting a New Roof

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting a new roof is a significant investment, and sidestepping common mistakes can save you a lot of stress and money. Here are a few pitfalls to watch out for.

First, don't rush the contractor selection. It's tempting to go with the first quote you get, but taking the time to research and get multiple bids is crucial. Look for licensed and insured contractors like Affordable Home Remodeling, who have a track record and local knowledge, especially in areas like Orange County and Los Angeles. We're licensed (No. 736771) and emphasize professionalism.

Second, avoid overlooking the warranty details. A good roof comes with material and labor warranties. Understand what's covered, for how long, and what might void it. Our company stands behind its work with labor warranties, ensuring peace of mind.

Third, don't skimp on materials. While budget is important, choosing the cheapest materials can lead to premature failure and more expenses down the road. Quality materials might cost more upfront but offer better durability, longevity, and often, better energy efficiency. Our team helps with value engineering, balancing cost and performance.

Fourth, don't ignore the importance of proper ventilation. A poorly ventilated attic can trap heat and moisture, shortening your roof's lifespan and increasing energy costs. A professional contractor will assess and address ventilation needs as part of the roofing project.

Fifth, don't neglect reading the contract thoroughly. Make sure the contract details the exact scope of work, materials to be used, payment schedule, cleanup procedures, and estimated timelines. Transparent contracts are a cornerstone of our operating philosophy, reducing misunderstandings.

Sixth, don't forget about ongoing maintenance. Even the best roof needs a little TLC. Regular inspections and cleaning of gutters can prevent small issues from becoming big problems. While our focus is installation, we advocate for homeowners understanding their role in maintaining their investment.
