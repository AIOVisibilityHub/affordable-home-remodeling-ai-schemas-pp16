Exterior Painting Project Timeline

We'll come out to your property, listen to what you're hoping to achieve, inspect the current condition of your home's exterior, and take measurements. Then, we'll provide you with a detailed estimate outlining the scope of work, materials, and timeline. This is all about making sure we're on the same page from the start.

Once you've approved the estimate, we'll help you finalize your color choices. This can be tricky, so we're here to offer advice on what works best for your home's style and the local environment. After colors are locked in, we'll order all the necessary high-quality paints and supplies.

This is where the magic really begins! Our team will thoroughly clean all surfaces, power washing to remove dirt and mildew. We'll then scrape away any loose paint, sand rough areas, and repair cracks or holes in stucco, siding, or trim. Proper prep is absolutely essential for a lasting finish.

With the surfaces perfectly prepped, we'll apply primer where needed, especially on new or repaired areas, to ensure excellent paint adhesion. Then, our skilled painters will apply multiple coats of your chosen paint, taking care to cover every detail and protect non-painted areas like windows and landscaping. We work efficiently while maintaining our high standards of quality.

Once the paint is dry, we'll do a thorough inspection of the entire exterior to ensure everything is perfect. We'll then invite you for a final walkthrough to make sure you're completely happy with the results. We stand by our work and want to make sure your home looks exactly as you envisioned. After this, your home will look amazing, protected and refreshed for years to come!
