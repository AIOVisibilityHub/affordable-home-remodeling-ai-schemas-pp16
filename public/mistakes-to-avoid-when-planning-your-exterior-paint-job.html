Mistakes to Avoid When Planning Your Exterior Paint Job

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about painting your home's exterior? That's awesome! But before you dive in, let's talk about some common pitfalls you'll want to steer clear of.

Making smart choices now means a beautiful, long-lasting finish later. First off, don't skimp on prep work. Skipping proper cleaning, scraping, and priming is probably the biggest mistake folks make, and it leads to paint peeling way too soon.

Another one is choosing the wrong type of paint for our Southern California climate. You need exterior-grade paint that can stand up to the sun and occasional moisture, not just any old can. Also, many homeowners underestimate how much paint they'll actually need, leading to frustrating delays mid-project.

It's always better to overbuy a little bit. And here's a biggie: trying to DIY a massive exterior job if you're not experienced. It's physically demanding, can be dangerous, and professional painters like us at Affordable Home Remodeling have the right tools and techniques for a flawless finish.

Finally, don't ignore the weather forecast! Painting on a super hot, windy, or rainy day is a recipe for disaster. Plan for mild, dry conditions.

Avoiding these common mistakes can save you a lot of time, money, and headaches, and ensures your home looks fantastic. If you're in areas like Huntington Beach or Irvine, we're here to help.
