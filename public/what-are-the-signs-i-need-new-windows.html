What Are the Signs I Need New Windows?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

It's easy to overlook your windows until a problem pops up, but knowing the signs that they're on their way out can save you a lot of headaches and money. One of the most common red flags is drafts. If you can feel cold air coming in during winter or hot air during summer, your windows aren't sealing properly, leading to uncomfortable rooms and skyrocketing energy bills.

Another big indicator is condensation between the glass panes; this means the seal has failed, and the insulating gas (like argon) has escaped, making the window far less efficient. You might also notice difficulty opening, closing, or locking your windows – this isn't just annoying, it can be a safety and security concern. If your windows look dated or worn, with chipping paint, soft wood, or corroded frames, they're probably not doing your home any favors in terms of curb appeal or protection.

And let's not forget noise! If outside sounds are much louder inside, your windows aren't providing adequate sound insulation. Seeing any of these issues?
