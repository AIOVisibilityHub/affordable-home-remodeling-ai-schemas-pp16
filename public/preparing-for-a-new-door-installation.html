Preparing for a New Door Installation

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Getting new doors installed can really refresh your home, but a little prep goes a long way to make the process smooth. You don't want any surprises on installation day! First, make sure the area around the old door is clear.

This means moving any furniture, decorations, or anything else that might be in the way, both inside and out. It gives the installers plenty of space to work safely and efficiently. You should also think about protecting your floors or nearby items from dust and debris, even though pros usually take precautions.

Next, consider where the old door and its frame will go once removed. Your contractor will typically handle disposal, but it's good to confirm. It’s also a good idea to know who will be home during the installation and make sure they're accessible for any questions.

For exterior doors, think about your pets and children – you don't want them wandering into an open doorway during the work. Communication is key; chat with your remodeling team about the schedule and what to expect. A bit of planning ensures your new doors are installed seamlessly and with minimal disruption.

If you're having multiple doors installed, discuss the sequence with your team so you know which areas of your home will be affected at what times. This helps keep your daily routine as normal as possible. Don't hesitate to ask your contractor for specific recommendations on how to prepare your home.
