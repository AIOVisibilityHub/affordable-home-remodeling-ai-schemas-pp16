What to Know Before Starting Interior Painting?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Ready to give your home a fresh look with interior painting? It's more than just picking a color! Knowing a few key things beforehand can make all the difference in achieving a professional finish.

First, consider the impact of lighting on your chosen colors. Natural and artificial light sources can dramatically change how a paint color appears throughout the day. It's smart to test swatches on your walls to see how they look in your specific space.

Second, proper preparation is crucial; this isn't a step you want to skip. Cleaning walls, filling holes, sanding rough spots, and priming are essential for paint adhesion and a smooth, even finish. Skipping these steps often leads to a less durable and aesthetically pleasing result.

Third, think about the type of paint and finish. Eggshell, satin, semi-gloss – each has its pros and cons regarding durability, washability, and appearance. Higher traffic areas might benefit from more durable, washable finishes.

Finally, consider if it's a DIY job or if professional help is warranted. For large areas, intricate trim, or if you're short on time, a professional painting service ensures quality, efficiency, and saves you the hassle.
