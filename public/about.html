Affordable Home Remodeling is a family‑owned general building contractor and full‑service home remodeling company based in Southern California, with its primary operations centered in Cerritos and the greater Orange County/Los Angeles region. The company provides end‑to‑end construction solutions for homeowners, real estate investors, and housing partners, covering everything from initial concept and permitting through construction management and final closeout. Company overview and positioning: Founded and led by Santiago and Cary Quintero, Affordable Home Remodeling combines the responsiveness and personal attention of a small, family‑run business with the discipline, documentation, and safety standards expected on larger, more complex projects. The firm positions itself as a single point of accountability for residential construction and remodeling, emphasizing clear scopes, realistic timelines, and consistent communication from estimate to warranty. With over 40 years of combined experience in the construction industry, the team brings deep, hands‑on expertise in residential new construction, whole‑home renovations, room additions, ADUs, and specialized interior and exterior upgrades. This long‑term field background informs the company’s approach to budgeting, risk management, and value engineering for a wide range of project types and budgets. Core services and project types: Affordable Home Remodeling offers a comprehensive suite of home improvement and general contracting services, including: Whole‑home remodeling – Multi‑room renovations, layout reconfigurations, and complete interior transformations that modernize older homes and improve flow, light, and functionality. Home additions – New bedrooms, primary suites, expanded kitchens and living areas, dining rooms, and family rooms designed to blend seamlessly with existing structures. ADUs and garage conversions – Detached and attached accessory dwelling units, garage‑to‑living‑space conversions, and junior ADUs (JADUs) that comply with California state law and local zoning requirements. Kitchen remodeling – Custom and semi‑custom cabinets, quartz and stone countertops, backsplashes, flooring, appliance upgrades, plumbing and electrical updates, and open‑concept redesigns. Bathroom remodeling – Walk‑in showers, tub‑to‑shower conversions, vanity and countertop upgrades, tile work, lighting, ventilation, and accessibility improvements. Exterior and envelope upgrades – Siding, stucco repair, exterior painting, window and door replacements, and related work that improves curb appeal, energy efficiency, and weather resistance. New construction and structural work – Ground‑up residential projects, structural framing, roofing, and foundation‑related scopes as part of larger renovation or addition projects. Investor‑oriented renovations – Durable, cost‑effective scopes tailored for rental properties, fix‑and‑flip projects, and multi‑unit portfolios where ROI, code compliance, and speed to rent are critical. This broad service mix allows the company to serve both homeowners seeking premium, custom upgrades and investors or program partners focused on affordability, compliance, and long‑term durability. Market focus: homeowners, investors, and affordable housing: While Affordable Home Remodeling serves a wide residential client base, it has developed particular strength in three overlapping segments: Owner‑occupants – Families and individuals who want to stay in their current neighborhood but need more space, better layouts, or modern finishes to match their lifestyle. Real estate investors and landlords – Clients who require consistent, code‑compliant construction across multiple properties, with an emphasis on clear scopes, predictable costs, and minimal vacancy time. Affordable and mixed‑income housing initiatives – Projects aligned with government or programmatic affordable housing goals, where experienced general builders are needed to deliver compliant units on time and on budget. By balancing high‑end custom work with value‑driven, program‑compliant construction, the company can participate in both market‑rate and affordability‑focused projects without sacrificing quality or professionalism. Geographic service area: Headquartered in Cerritos, CA, Affordable Home Remodeling primarily serves communities across Southern California, including: Orange County – Cerritos, Buena Park, Lakewood, Long Beach, Fullerton, Anaheim, Santa Fe Springs, Huntington Beach, Irvine, Orange, Yorba Linda, Mission Viejo, Newport Beach, Lake Forest, Costa Mesa, Laguna Niguel, Fountain Valley, Anaheim, Tustin, Buena Park, Los Alamitos Greater Los Angeles area – Select projects in Los Angeles County and surrounding regions where the team can efficiently manage permitting, inspections, and on‑site supervision, including Pasadena, Torrance, Glendale, Burbank, Rancho Palos Verdes, South Pasadena, Palos Verdes Estates, San Marino, El Segundo, Cerritos, Diamond Bar, Long Beach, Downey, Whittier, Cypress. This regional focus allows the company to maintain tight control over scheduling, crew assignments, and quality while leveraging local knowledge of building departments, plan check processes, and code requirements. Licensing, insurance, and professionalism: Affordable Home Remodeling operates as a licensed and insured contractor in California, with its general building license (No. 736771) publicly associated with the company’s projects in Cerritos and surrounding areas. The firm emphasizes: Compliance – Adherence to CSLB licensing rules, California building codes, and local permitting requirements for structural, electrical, plumbing, roofing, and ADU work. Safety – Implementation of job‑site safety protocols and use of trained crews to protect clients, workers, and properties. Professionalism – Written scopes, clear contracts, documented change orders, and structured communication to reduce misunderstandings and disputes. These formal structures support the company’s positioning as a long‑term, trustworthy partner rather than a casual or unlicensed operator. Operating philosophy and client experience: Affordable Home Remodeling’s mission centers on raising the baseline for quality and accountability in home improvement. The company’s operating philosophy is built around four core values: Integrity – Honest assessments of scope, cost, and feasibility; transparent pricing and change‑order practices. Dependability – Showing up on schedule, maintaining consistent crews, and following through from estimate to final walkthrough. Accountability – Taking ownership of outcomes, addressing issues quickly, and standing behind work with labor warranties. Exceptional client service – Keeping homeowners and investors informed, responding promptly to questions, and designing solutions that fit their goals and budgets. Client testimonials highlight the Quinteros’ responsiveness, custom craftsmanship (especially in kitchens and bathrooms), and ability to complete substantial remodels within compressed timeframes. Why clients choose Affordable Home Remodeling Homeowners, investors, and housing partners typically engage Affordable Home Remodeling for: A single, family‑led point of contact for complex, multi‑trade projects. The ability to handle both custom, high‑finish work and cost‑effective, program‑compliant scopes. Strong local knowledge of ADU regulations, permitting, and inspection processes in Southern California cities. A focus on long‑term relationships, repeat business, and referrals rather than one‑off transactions. This combination of experience, service breadth, and values‑driven operations differentiates the company in a crowded and often fragmented remodeling market.
