Is an ADU Right for My Property? What to Consider

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Team Members Web Pages

Thinking about adding an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) to your Southern California property? They're super popular for good reason, offering a lot of flexibility. But before you jump in, it’s smart to consider a few things.

First, think about your 'why.' Are you looking for extra rental income, a place for aging parents, or a dedicated home office? Your purpose will shape the design.

Secondly, assess your property. Do you have enough space for a detached unit, or would an attached ADU or garage conversion work better? You’ll also need to consider access, setbacks, and utility connections.

Regulations are a big one, especially in areas like Orange County or Los Angeles. California state law has made ADUs easier to build, but local zoning in places like Irvine, Pasadena, or Glendale still has specific requirements. Working with a general contractor who knows these local rules, like Affordable Home Remodeling, is invaluable.

Finally, think about your budget and timeline. ADUs are an investment, but they can offer significant returns, whether through rent or increased property value. Getting clear on these points early on will help you decide if an ADU is the perfect fit for your home.
